📱 How to Subscribe

🍏 Apple Calendar (iOS / macOS)

  1. Click Subscribe next to your county.
  2. Tap Subscribe when prompted by Calendar.
  3. Set Auto-refresh to Every 15 minutes or Every hour.

📬 Microsoft Outlook

  1. Click Copy URL next to your county.
  2. In Outlook, go to Add Calendar → Subscribe from web.
  3. Paste the URL and click Import.

🌐 Google Calendar

  1. Click Copy URL next to your county.
  2. In Google Calendar, click + next to Other calendars → From URL.
  3. Paste the URL and click Add calendar.
⚠️ Important Calendar Client Notice:
Apple Calendar and Outlook allow you to configure refresh intervals down to 15–30 minutes.
However, Google Calendar polls external calendar subscriptions on its own schedule (typically every 8 to 24 hours), which cannot be changed by feed providers or users. If you rely on time-sensitive weather warnings, Apple Calendar, Outlook, or native calendar apps with customizable refresh intervals are recommended.
